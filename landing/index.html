A natural voice

Pronunciation comes from your phone’s own speech engine. Neural voices when you’re online, the best local voice when you’re not. No audio files, no bloat.

Installing takes about a minute.

Download the APK on your Android phone and open it.

Allow the install if Android asks (Settings → Install unknown apps, for your browser).

Updating works the same way. Install the new file over the old one and your progress stays.

Want to check the file? On a computer, run sha256sum on it. The result should match the checksum above.

Questions people ask.

Is the APK safe?

Yes. It’s built from the project’s source, signed with a single private key and hosted on GitHub Releases. Each version lists its SHA-256 checksum, and Android refuses to update the app with a file signed by anyone else.

Why isn’t it on Google Play?

Direct downloads let new versions reach you right away, without store fees or review delays. The app stays small, independent and free of ads.

Is there an iPhone version?

It’s in development. For now, Vocab is available for Android.

Does it need the internet?

Only to sign in and to download sets. After that, learning, voice and progress all work offline.

What happens to my data?

Your progress, saved words and known words stay on your phone. Google sign-in is only used to unlock downloads. No ads, no trackers.

The voice sounds robotic.

Open Settings in the app and tap Better voices, then install the high-quality voice data for Korean or English.
