So the words don’t.

One word on a black screen. Three quiet buttons. Nothing between you and memory.

01

Hold to peek

Press and hold anywhere to flip the card. Lift your finger and it turns back — your brain does the work, not a button.

02

Download a set once and it lives in your phone’s storage. Subway, plane, mountain — every word still works.

03

Natural voice

Pronunciation from your phone’s own speech engine — neural voices when online, the best local voice when not. No audio files, no bloat.

04

One endless line

Every word you download flows into a single deck that loops forever. Or pick one set when you want focus.

05

05 — Questions

Good questions.

Is the APK safe?

Yes. It’s built from the project’s source, signed with a single private key, and hosted on GitHub Releases. Each version lists its SHA-256 checksum — if the numbers match, the file is exactly what was published. Android also refuses to update the app with a file signed by anyone else.

Why isn’t it on Google Play?

Direct downloads let new versions reach you immediately, without store fees or review delays. The app stays small, ad-free and independent.

Does it need the internet?

Only to sign in and to download sets. After that, learning, voice and progress all work fully offline.

What happens to my data?

Your progress, saved words and known words stay on your phone. Google sign-in is used only to unlock downloads. No ads, no trackers, no selling anything.

How do I update?

Download the newest version here and install it over the old one. Your words and progress stay. Vocabulary sets update by themselves inside the app.

The voice sounds robotic.

Open the app’s Settings → Better voices, and install high-quality voice data for Korean or English from Android’s speech settings.
